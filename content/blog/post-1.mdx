IoT devices are notoriously vulnerable. In this post, I discuss how to implement application-layer encryption (AES-128) on ESP32 devices to secure BLE communications, based on my recent project.

### Why Application-Layer?
Transport layer security is often not enough for edge devices. Application-layer encryption guarantees end-to-end security, even if the intermediate nodes are compromised.
